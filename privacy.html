Thinking in CARDS is built to be local-first. Your projects live in your browser, on your device, and we want them to stay yours.

This policy explains what information we handle, where it goes, and what choices you have. It applies to this website, to the Thinking in CARDS modeling application (previously called OOUX Tool, available at OOUX.cards), and to the related tools described in our Terms of Service.

1The short version

  • Your projects are saved in your browser by default. We do not receive them unless you sign in or connect an AI agent.
  • Signing in currently saves your projects to our cloud database. We are changing this so that storing projects in the cloud is always a separate, explicit choice. Until then, if you want your projects to stay only on your device, use the app without an account.
  • AI agents you connect can read your open project, and change it if you allow that. Their requests and the answers pass through our relay service, which forwards them and is not designed to keep them.
  • Usage information, such as which pages are visited and which features are used, helps us run and improve the service. It is designed not to include the content of your projects.
  • Waitlist emails are used only to tell you when Thinking in CARDS is ready.

3Your projects

On your device. When you use the app without an account, your projects are saved in your browser’s storage on your device. Their content, meaning the objects, attributes, relationships, notes and everything else you model, is not sent to our servers for storage.

You stay in control of that local copy. You can export a project as a file at any time and keep it wherever you like, and you can delete a project in the app. Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or switching browsers or devices can make local projects unavailable, so please keep your own backups, as described in our Terms of Service.

If you sign in. When you create an account and sign in, the app currently saves your projects to our cloud database, provided by Supabase, so that they are linked to your account. Projects already stored in your browser at that moment are copied to your account as well.

Where we are heading. Our principle is that your projects stay on your device unless you clearly choose to store them remotely, and that creating an account or signing in should never count as that choice by itself. We are updating the app to work this way, and we will update this policy when it does.

Some features send project information to another place for a task you start, as described in section 4. That is temporary processing rather than storage, but we mention it here because project content does leave your device.

4Connecting an AI agent (MCP)

You can choose to connect an AI agent, such as an AI assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), to a project open in your browser. Nothing is shared with an agent unless you set up this connection.

When an agent is connected:

  • The agent sends its requests to our relay service, which passes them to your open browser tab. Your tab carries out each request on the project it has loaded.
  • The answer travels back through the relay to the agent. Answers can include project content, such as object names, attributes, relationships and other details the agent asked for.
  • The relay handles this information in memory, only to pass it along, and is not designed to store project content or keep copies of answers. It keeps short-lived connection details, such as session information and a protected form of your connection key, which expire after a limited time.
  • The agent, and the company that provides it, receive whatever the agent reads from your project. How they use it is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Choose “Read only” access if you do not want an agent to change your project.

Starting an agent session may require an account. Where usage limits apply to agent connections, we keep a count of the requests made by your account, with technical details such as the type of request and when it happened. This count does not include project content.

Temporary project copies. Some features that hand a project from one session to another can upload a temporary copy of it to private cloud storage. These copies are set to expire shortly after they are created, after which they can no longer be opened.

5Accounts

You can use Thinking in CARDS without an account. If you create one, we process your email address and a password to sign you in. Accounts are provided through Supabase Auth, which handles password storage. Each account also has a unique account ID, which identifies it in the systems described in this policy.

To close your account, or to ask about deleting information linked to it, contact us at the address in section 14.

6Usage information

Website and app visits. We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how this website and the app are used. It records page views together with basic technical information about each visit, such as the page, the referring website, the type of browser, operating system and device, and an approximate location derived from the connection. Vercel’s documentation describes how it processes this information.

Product events in the app. The app can send product usage events to PostHog. These record that something happened, for example that a project was created, opened, imported or exported, that a part of the app was opened, or that an AI agent connected or made a request, along with simple categories such as the file format or whether the action succeeded. They are designed not to include project content, project or file names, or anything you type. Session recording and automatic capture of what you click are turned off.

To group events from the same browser, the app stores a random identifier in your browser. If you are signed in, events are linked to your account ID instead, never to your name or email address.

Cookies and browser storage. This website’s own code does not set cookies. The app uses your browser’s storage to keep your projects, your settings and the random identifier mentioned above.

Like any website, our hosting provider processes technical information such as your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages and protect the service.

7Feedback

If you use the “Give feedback” form in the app, we receive the feedback you write and, only if you choose to provide them, your name, email address and type of work. This is sent only when you submit the form, through our product analytics provider, PostHog. No project content, screenshot or recording is attached.

8The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, we receive the email address you enter. It is delivered to our inbox as an email through Resend, an email delivery service. We use it only to tell you when Thinking in CARDS is ready.

You can ask to be removed from the waitlist at any time by writing to the address in section 14.

9Service providers

We rely on the following providers. Each one handles information for the purpose listed, under its own privacy policy.

  • Vercel: hosting of this website and the app, website analytics, and private storage for temporary project copies. Vercel privacy policy
  • Supabase: accounts and sign-in, and storage of projects for signed-in users. Supabase privacy policy
  • PostHog: product usage events and feedback in the app. PostHog privacy policy
  • Resend: delivery of waitlist signups to our inbox. Resend privacy policy
  • Google (Gmail): the inbox where waitlist signups and messages to us arrive. Google privacy policy
  • Cloud hosting for the MCP relay: runs the relay service described in section 4.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

10How long information is kept

  • Projects in your browser stay there until you delete them or clear your browser data. We cannot see them or delete them for you.
  • Projects saved to your account stay in your account until they are deleted. You can contact us to ask for this.
  • Agent connections: relay session details are kept in memory and expire after a limited time.
  • Temporary project copies expire shortly after they are created, as described in section 4.
  • Waitlist addresses are kept for the release announcement, and you can ask to be removed at any time.
  • Usage events and feedback are kept by our analytics providers according to the retention settings of their services.

11Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account, so your projects stay only on your device.
  • Export your projects whenever you like.
  • Do not connect an AI agent, or connect one with “Read only” access.
  • Block analytics with your browser’s privacy settings or a content blocker. The app keeps working.
  • Ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address in section 14.

Depending on where you live, data protection law may give you further rights over your personal information.

12Children

Thinking in CARDS is not designed for children. If you are under the age of legal majority where you live, please use it only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian, as set out in our Terms of Service.
